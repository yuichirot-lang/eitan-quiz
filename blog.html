Cafe Maria

🏝️Honolulu
👩‍🍳Maria

Cafe Maria is my favorite restaurant in Honolulu. The restaurant does not have a website, but it is very popular. Maria is the owner of the restaurant. She does not speak Japanese, but she knows a lot about Japan. She is kind and friendly.

My favorite dish there is loco moco. It is a beef patty and a fried egg on top of rice. It is wonderful.
